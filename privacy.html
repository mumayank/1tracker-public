Privacy

Weight stays on your phone unless you choose otherwise.

On your device

Weigh-ins are stored locally on your Android phone. You can use 1Tracker without an account.

Optional Google Drive

If you connect Drive, 1Tracker can back up a JSON snapshot of your weight history to your own hidden Drive app data folder. We don’t operate a 1Tracker server that reads that file.

Updates

The app can check a public GitHub file for a newer APK. That check does not send your weight data.
