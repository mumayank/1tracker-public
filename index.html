Privacy & terms

Weigh-ins and blood sugar readings stay on your phone. 1Tracker does not create an account, show ads, or send your numbers to us. A readable JSON backup (weigh-ins + readings) leaves the device only if you share it. Update checks download a version file (and an APK if you tap install) from GitHub.

The app is free, not medical advice, and provided as-is — you install and use it at your own risk. Using 1Tracker means you agree to this. Contact us.
