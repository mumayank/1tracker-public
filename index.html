How 1Tracker works

  • Offline only

    Logging never needs a network. A new version uses the network only if you tap to check.

  • 100% free

    No account, no ads, no in-app payments. You never pay to use 1Tracker.

  • Your data stays yours

    Weigh-ins live on your phone. We don’t see them, sell them, or process them.

  • We never look

    There is no account and no cloud copy of your numbers unless you share a backup yourself.

  • Not medical advice

    1Tracker is provided as-is. You install and use it at your own risk. Using it means you agree to this.
