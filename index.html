Gym. Gloves. Go.

Huge save buttons. Thumb-first layout. Haptics when it counts. One job per screen — log, then leave.

Readable JSON. You keep it.

Your weigh-ins export as plain JSON you can open in any editor. Share it to Files, Drive, or wherever you want — 1Tracker never auto-uploads it.

Privacy & terms

Weigh-ins stay on your phone. 1Tracker does not create an account, show ads, or send your numbers to us. A readable JSON backup leaves the device only if you share it. Update checks download a version file (and an APK if you tap install) from GitHub. The app is free, not medical advice, and provided as-is — you install and use it at your own risk.
