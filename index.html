Blood sugar, without the guesswork.

Log a reading and say when you took it — fasting, before a meal, or two hours after. 1Tracker places you on a colored spectrum: normal, prediabetes, or diabetes range, using the same bands doctors use. The bands shift with the timing, because that is how the numbers actually work.

Gym. Gloves. Go.

Huge save buttons while today's weigh-in is pending — then the log button quietly shrinks to a pill and the chart gets the room. Thumb-first, haptics when it counts, one job per screen.

A goal with a date.

Set a goal weight and 1Tracker projects when your trend gets you there — honestly. Flat trend or moving the wrong way? It says so instead of inventing a date.

Know your healthy range.

Add your height once and see your BMI with the healthy weight range for your body. A range, never a verdict — one tap hides it for good.

Water weight, filtered.

A dashed smoothed-trend line rides over the chart, so a salty dinner doesn't read as a setback. The raw dots stay honest.

Editable entries.

Made a typo? Tap any weigh-in in your history to fix the weight or change the date and time. Your data, your control.

Readable JSON. You keep it.

Your weigh-ins export as plain JSON you can open in any editor. Share it to Files, Drive, or wherever you want — 1Tracker never auto-uploads it. Restore from a backup anytime.

Morning reminder.

Optional 8 AM notification to weigh in on an empty stomach. Dismissed? It comes back in 15 minutes until you log. Fully optional — deny and the app never asks again.

Day-to-day delta.

Every entry shows yesterday's change in kg — green when you're down, red when you're up. The more you log, the clearer the picture.

Offline only.

Every weigh-in is on-device. No account required to start. Updates download in-app when you're ready.

One tap per timing.

Fasting, before a meal, two hours after, or any other time. Open it in the morning and Fasting is already picked. Big tappable cards, no typing unless you want to.

Averages that mean something.

Fasting and after-meal averages for the last 7 and 30 days, plus the share of readings in the normal range — unlocked after three readings, never faked before. Filter the trend to one timing for the cleanest line.

A 7 AM nudge. Only if you want it.

An optional fasting reminder before breakfast, and it stays quiet once today's fasting reading is in. mg/dL or mmol/L, switch anytime. Friendly guidance with every reading — never a diagnosis.

Privacy & terms

Weigh-ins and blood sugar readings stay on your phone. 1Tracker does not create an account, show ads, or send your numbers to us. A readable JSON backup leaves the device only if you share it. Update checks download a version file (and an APK if you tap install) from GitHub. Reminder notifications are local only — nothing is sent anywhere. Sugar guidance uses public ADA and WebMD references and is not medical advice or a diagnosis; confirm anything important with your doctor. The app is free and provided as-is — you install and use it at your own risk. Using 1Tracker means you agree to this. Contact us.
